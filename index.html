From Zack & Christen

A decade of Paso Robles, brought home.

We've been visiting Paso Robles wine country for more than ten years now — and every trip, we find something new: another vineyard, another hiking trail, another bottle worth remembering. This fall, we wanted to bring a little of that home and share it with you.

One of our favorite wineries, Seven Oxen Estate Wines, has chosen our home as their next Wine Member "Road Trip" stop. So we're setting a long table in the backyard, stringing up some lights, and inviting the people we love.

They bring the wine.
We provide the table.
You bring the company.
