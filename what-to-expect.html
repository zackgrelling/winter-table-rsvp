Sunday, November 15 · An Evening at Our Table

What to Expect

Dinner · Wine · Charcuterie & Cheeses · Friends

30+ friends. One very long U-shaped table. Great food. Wonderful wine. No agenda beyond having a really good evening together.

We're so excited to have you join us.

We've invited our friends from Seven Oxen Estate Wines in Paso Robles to join us for the evening and share some of their favorite wines with us. They'll be weaving the wine experience into dinner, telling a few stories, pouring some great wines, and helping us discover what pairs best with what's on our table.

But most importantly…

…it's dinner with friends.

The Flow

How the evening unfolds.

Seven tastings in all — three before dinner, two with the barbecue, two with dessert — each one poured and introduced by Seven Oxen Estate Wines. You'll each get a tasting card to track your favorites along the way.

①
4:00 PM Wine & Charcuterie Tastings 1–3

Arrive & Unwind

Come whenever you're ready and settle in.

4:15 PM

The first of seven tastings begins at 4:15 PM, with three poured here alongside hors d'oeuvres and charcuterie. Grab a glass, find a comfortable spot, say hello to some familiar faces and meet someone new.

Take your time. The evening starts with simply enjoying one another.

②
5:00 PM Dinner Begins Tastings 4–5

Find Your Seat

When it's time, we'll invite everyone to gather around our U-shaped table. We'll welcome everyone, share a few words, and introduce the Seven Oxen Estate Wines team before dinner begins.

Then… we eat.

③
Time TBD Barbecue + Reds

Pour · Taste · Enjoy

This is the heart of the evening. Our main course is a barbecue of assorted meats, paired with a couple of red wine tastings — bringing us to five of the seven. As dinner progresses, Seven Oxen Estate Wines will introduce each pour and share the stories, flavors and pairings behind them.

They'll be moving around the table and interacting with everyone, so ask questions, compare notes, discover a new favorite—or simply enjoy the wine in your glass.

There is nothing to study. Nothing to memorize. Just something new to taste.

④
Time TBD Dessert + Final Pours

Stay Awhile

As dinner winds down, we'll move into the final two tastings of the night, paired with dessert — seven in all. Take a look at your tasting card: by now you'll know which pours were your favorites. If somewhere along the way you discover a wine you absolutely love, Seven Oxen Estate Wines will have an opportunity for you to learn about taking a few bottles home and/or becoming part of their wine club.

That part is completely optional. We invited Seven Oxen Estate Wines because we genuinely love their wines and wanted to share something special with our friends. If you find a bottle you love, wonderful. If you don't, that's wonderful too.

We'd love for you to buy something and support our friends at Seven Oxen Estate Wines — a boutique winery that traveled all the way down to give us a great night — but there's no expectation.

Before You Come

The only things we ask.

  • Come hungry.
  • Come thirsty.
  • Come comfortable.
  • Come ready to linger.

We'll take care of the rest.

We're really looking forward to spending the evening with you.

Good food. Good wine. Great friends.

See you Sunday.
